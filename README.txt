Pocket Budget - install on iPhone

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole "pocket-budget" folder onto the page (free, no coding). You'll get a web address.
2. Open that address in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The app opens full screen with its own icon and works offline.
Live prices: add a free Twelve Data API key (twelvedata.com) in the Accounts tab. For Canadian TSX stocks, also add a free Alpha Vantage key (alphavantage.co) and enter symbols ending in .TO, like RY.TO.
AI assistant and morning brief: add a free Gemini API key (aistudio.google.com) in the Accounts tab. It also checks prices with Google Search for any stock the other services miss.
Receipt scanning: reads receipts on your phone. The reader downloads once (a few MB) the first time you scan, then works offline.
Your data stays on your phone. To move data from the Claude version, tap "Back up data" there (Accounts tab), then "Restore from backup" here.
